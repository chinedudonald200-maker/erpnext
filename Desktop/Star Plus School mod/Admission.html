Admission Requirements

📋

Documentation

  • Birth Certificate (original & photocopy)
  • Parent/Guardian ID
  • Previous School Report Card
  • Medical Report/Vaccination Certificate
  • Passport Photograph (2x2)
✅

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: Must be 3+ years for Nursery
  • Age: Must be 6+ years for Primary 1
  • Good health condition
  • No serious behavioral issues
  • School compatibility assessment
💰

Financial Requirements

  • Application Fee: ₦5,000
  • Registration Fee: ₦15,000
  • First Term Fees (Tuition)
  • PTA/Development Levy
  • Flexible payment plans available
🎯

Assessment Process

  • English Language Test
  • Mathematics Aptitude Test
  • Reasoning & Logic Assessment
  • One-on-one Interview
  • Parent Interview Session

Admission Steps

1

Submit Application

Complete the online admission form with required personal and family information. Application fee: ₦5,000

2

Document Verification

Submit all required documents for verification. Our admission team will review and confirm completeness within 3 business days.

3

Entrance Assessment

Your child will take entrance exams in English, Mathematics, and Reasoning. Assessment scheduled at your convenience.

4

Parent Interview

Meet with our admission officer to discuss child's needs, school expectations, and answer any questions you may have.

5

Admission Decision

Receive admission status within 5 business days. Successful applicants receive admission letter and further instructions.

6

Enrollment

Pay registration fee and complete enrollment process. Receive school uniform, books, and orientation schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

When can I apply for admission? +

Applications are accepted throughout the year. However, peak admission is between November and March for the following academic session. Early applications are encouraged for better class selection.

What is the average class size? +

Nursery classes have a maximum of 25 students, while Primary classes have a maximum of 35 students. This ensures personalized attention and effective teaching.

Do you offer scholarship programs? +

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships for exceptional students and need-based scholarships for deserving families. Contact our admission office for details.

What are the school timings? +

School hours are 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM (Nursery) and 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM (Primary). Extended care is available until 5:00 PM for an additional fee.

Can I pay fees in installments? +

Yes, flexible payment plans are available. You can pay fees in quarterly installments. Speak with our finance department for customized payment arrangements.

What is your refund/withdrawal policy? +

Refund policies vary based on when withdrawal occurs. Withdrawals before the academic session begins are fully refundable. Please refer to our admission letter for detailed policy.
