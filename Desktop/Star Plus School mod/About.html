Our Journey

1999

Founded

STARPLUS INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL was established with a vision to provide quality education to primary school students in a nurturing environment.

2005

Expansion

Added new classrooms and modern facilities to accommodate growing student population and enhance learning experience.

2012

Technology Integration

Introduced computer labs and digital learning platforms to revolutionize teaching and learning methodologies.

2020

Digital Transformation

Successfully transitioned to hybrid learning during pandemic, implementing virtual classrooms and online assessment systems.

2024

Innovation Excellence

Achieved recognition as one of the best primary schools with AI-powered learning and secure digital infrastructure.
